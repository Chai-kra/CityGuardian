# CityGuardian
<p>AI City Guardian is an AI-powered civic reporting platform that allows residents to submit infrastructure complaints with photos, locations, descriptions, and landmarks; AI analyses the image and location to identify the issue, determine its priority and responsible department, and route it to the nearest suitable branch. The system groups duplicate reports within a 50-metre area, while department administrators can filter cases by branch, view them on a map, manually correct assignments, update statuses, and send feedback notifications to users. Citizens can track their reports from pending to underway and settled, departments can monitor analytics and performance, and national administrators have a complete nationwide view for reviewing and reassigning cases when necessary.</p>
